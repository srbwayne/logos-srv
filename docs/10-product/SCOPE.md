# Scope

## In scope for modernization

- preserve and characterize the current progression behavior;
- isolate progression calculations from orchestration and infrastructure;
- reduce coupling between domain and application/web/auth concerns;
- establish external subject identity instead of duplicating personal identity;
- expose a stable progression contract;
- integrate one LifeOS vertical in shadow mode before authority cutover.

## Out of scope until explicitly approved

- big-bang rewrite;
- reimplementation in Python;
- Kafka or another broker solely for architectural purity;
- Kubernetes/service mesh;
- replacing PostgreSQL without evidence;
- merging Logos and LifeOS repositories;
- allowing AI/LLM output to determine canonical XP directly.
